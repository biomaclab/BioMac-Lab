# 🧬 BioMac Lab

> Hub of Future-Oriented Genomic Research and Bioinformatics Innovation

Welcome to **BioMac Lab** (Bioinformatics and Macromolecular Analysis Center) - a cutting-edge research organization dedicated to advancing genomic research, bioinformatics, and computational biology.

## 🔬 About Us

BioMac Lab is at the forefront of biological innovation, combining advanced computational techniques with genomic research to unlock new insights in life sciences. Our interdisciplinary team of researchers, bioinformaticians, and data scientists work together to solve complex biological problems through innovative computational approaches.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To accelerate biological discovery through:
- **Advanced Genomic Analysis**: Developing novel methods for genome sequencing and analysis
- **Computational Biology**: Creating innovative bioinformatics tools and algorithms
- **Open Science**: Sharing our research, tools, and data with the scientific community
- **Collaborative Research**: Fostering partnerships across disciplines and institutions

## 🔍 Research Focus Areas

### Genomics & Sequencing
- Next-generation sequencing (NGS) analysis
- Genome assembly and annotation
- Comparative genomics
- Metagenomics and microbiome analysis

### Bioinformatics Tools & Methods
- Algorithm development for biological data analysis
- Machine learning applications in genomics
- Structural biology and protein analysis
- Phylogenetics and evolutionary biology

### Data Science & Visualization
- Large-scale biological data processing
- Interactive visualization tools
- Database development and management
- Statistical analysis and modeling
